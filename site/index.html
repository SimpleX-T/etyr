Everything the dictionary gives you

Read deeper, faster.

Select. That’s it.

Highlight any word on any site. A sleek tooltip appears at the selection with the definition — no tabs, no searching, no leaving the page.

The full dictionary surface

Word forms, every example, quoted passages with citations, nested subsenses, and clickable synonym & antonym chips — aggregated across the whole entry, not just the first line.

Words that talk to each other

“Form of” senses and synonym chips are live links. Click decades and jump straight to decade — inside the same tooltip, with a back button for your path.

Copy anything, cited

Copy a single definition, a quote with its citation, or the whole entry — formatted and ready to paste into notes, essays, or your own dictionary.

A side panel for long reads

Send any word to the side panel and keep it open while you keep reading. Once it’s open, every word you select lands there — with a search box of your own.

Build your vocabulary

Bookmark words, re-visit your lookups, and study them later. Everything is local — no account, no profile, no tracking.

AI Fallback

Encountered slang, a modern acronym, or an obscure term? Etyr uses an advanced AI search algorithm to define words not found in traditional dictionaries.

Beautiful New Tab

Etyr transforms your new tab page into a customizable dashboard featuring your saved vocabulary and a daily curated word to keep your vocabulary sharp.

Local-first by design

Your reading stays yours.

No accounts.

Etyr has no sign-in, no profile, and nothing to “sync” unless you say so.

No tracking.

The extension never collects page content, browsing history, or your bookmarks.

Only the word leaves.

When you look up a word, that word is sent to a public dictionary API. Nothing else.

Everything else is local.

Saved words, lookups, and settings live in your browser’s local storage.
